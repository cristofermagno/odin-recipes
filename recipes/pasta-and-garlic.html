Pasta and Garlic

Description

An easy recipe to prepare of pasta and garlic. The presence of the golden brown garlic gives this recipe a unique flavor. It's perfect for those who don't have experience in the kitchen, and it's quick to make.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 pounds pasta
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed, or more to taste
  • salt and pepper to taste
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. In a large pot of salted water boil paste until al dente (neither too hard nor too soft). Drain well.
  2. In a small saucepan heat oil slightly, add garlic. Sauce garlic until it is lightly browned. Be Careful not to burn garlic.
  3. Combine pasta and garlic, salt and pepper to taste. Top with Parmesan or Romano cheese and serve.
