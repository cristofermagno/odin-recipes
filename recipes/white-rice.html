White Rice

Description

A simple and classical recipe of white rice easy to learn. The rice is a side dish that could be used with a variety of dishes.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain white rice
  • 2 tablespoons minced onion
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups hot water

Steps

  1. Place the rice in colander and rinse thoroughly with cold water, set aside
  2. Heat the oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Cook the onion in the oil for one minute
  3. Stir in the garlic and cook until the garlic is golden brown.
  4. Add the rice and salt and cook and stir until begins to brown.
  5. Pour hot water over rice mixture and stir. Reduce heat to low, cover the saucepan, and allow to simmer until the water has been absorbed, 20 to 25 minutes
